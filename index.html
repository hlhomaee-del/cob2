Electronic Claims & Submission

Understand how claims are submitted electronically and why the format matters.

Key Concepts

ANSI ASC X12 837

The standardized electronic format for submitting healthcare claims. Required under HIPAA for electronic transactions.

Electronic Media Claim (EMC)

Any claim submitted electronically rather than on paper — the standard in modern billing.

Value-Added Network (VAN)

A private network that facilitates EDI transactions between trading partners — acts as a secure data highway for claim transmission.

Covered Entity

Under HIPAA: health plans, healthcare clearinghouses, and providers who transmit electronic transactions. Must follow HIPAA standards.

Clearinghouse, EDI & Claim Files

Understand how claims are scrubbed, converted, and transmitted.

The Clearinghouse Role

A clearinghouse is an intermediary that receives electronic claims from providers, scrubs them for errors, reformats them to meet payer-specific requirements, and transmits them to the appropriate payer. Clearinghouses must meet accreditation standards and comply with HIPAA as covered entities.

EDI = Electronic Data Interchange
The standardized electronic exchange of data between healthcare entities. EDI eliminates paper and ensures consistent formatting across different systems.

Coordination of Benefits (COB)

When a patient has more than one insurance plan, COB rules determine which payer is primary and which is secondary. The primary payer processes first, and the secondary considers what remains. COB prevents overpayment — the combined payments should not exceed the allowed amount.

Remittance, EOB/ERA & Payment Thinking

Learn to interpret what the payer tells you about their payment decision.

ERA vs EOB

Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA)

The electronic version of the payment explanation sent to the provider. Uses ANSI X12 835 format. Contains what was paid, denied, adjusted, and why.

Explanation of Benefits (EOB)

The paper explanation sent to the patient showing what the insurance paid, what the patient owes, and why. Similar information as ERA but patient-facing.
